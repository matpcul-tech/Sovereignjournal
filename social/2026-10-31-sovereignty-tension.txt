If you don't own the hardware, someone else's lender owns your AI.

The neocloud financing model is hitting its limit, and it's happening in public. Debt markets have largely closed to pure-play GPU cloud providers without substantial non-AI revenue. Gartner projects roughly 80% of independent neoclouds will lose their independence by 2030.

That is counterparty risk. It shows up the moment the entity running your models doesn't own the hardware.

A single containerized node, owned by the organization that uses the compute and holds the data, removes that exposure. Capital is spent once. The asset stays on the owner's balance sheet. Governance lives on hardware the owner controls.

Borrowed rails run fast. They just don't run yours.
