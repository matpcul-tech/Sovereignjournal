## Core constructs
- Inclusive Adaptive Capacity: an organization's ability to adapt is bounded by how widely it includes voices before adapting.
- Participatory Sensemaking: meaning is made collectively, and decisions made with the room hold longer than decisions made for it.
- Equity-Centered Flexibility: flexibility that does not center equity redistributes advantage to whoever was already ahead.
- Psychological Safety as mediator: safety is the channel the other three constructs travel through, not the destination.

## Extension constructs (The Sovereign Stack, July 2026)
- Sovereignty Tension: the pull between controlling your own infrastructure and moving at the speed of borrowed infrastructure.
- Cost of Consistency: every rule an organization refuses to bend has a price; leadership is knowing the bill before paying it.
